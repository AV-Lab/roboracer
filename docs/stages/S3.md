## 2. The C0 plan

- **Reference:** the collider-derived centerline, 313 points at 0.1 m, lap 31.32 m; it starts at the spawn in the driving direction.
- **Line:** A2RL min-curvature line, 0.3 m margin from each edge, start pinned within ±0.05 m.
  - It moves at most **0.11 m** from the centerline, and the lap shortens to 31.25 m. On this narrow track (1.14–2.55 m wide) the centerline is already close to the min-curvature line.
  - Polyline clearance to the edge is 0.48 m.
  - An exactly converged solve (tighter solver tolerances, or the geometry scaled ×37 to A2RL's spacing) gives 0.113–0.114 m, so the default solver stopping slightly early does not matter.
- **Why the spacing must stay fine:** at 0.5 m spacing the line seemed to gain more (offset 0.49 m), but its segments cut the inner edges, to 0.26 m inside a 0.3 m margin, while every point met it. The plan builder now refuses such lines.
- **Truth isolation:** the plan is built from the S1-05 collider geometry, the same source as the scorer's. S3-01 still has to make the driving map a separate, validated asset (S3-04).

## 3. No-motion evidence

Evidence: `artifacts/s3/20261022T110052Z-no-motion-a2rl-port/`.

**Offline shadow on a recorded S2 lap** (`scripts/roboracer_s3_shadow.py`; open-loop, IPS pose, D32 bring-up):
- A's lap runs a median 0.24 m (max 0.87 m) from the racing line, so B's commands along A's path are mostly corrections (safe-stop thresholds exceeded on 710 of 1,391 ticks).
- Open-loop shadowing therefore says little about B's own driving. The closed-loop check below does.

**Closed-loop kinematic check** (`scripts/roboracer_s3_closedloop_sim.py`):
- **Model:** B on the C0 plan driving a kinematic bicycle with the measured AutoDRIVE timing: a new pose every 0.18 s, 0.3 s old (and 0.345 s, the S2 maximum); a 0.1 s tick; steering slew 183°/s outward, instant toward zero; footprint-to-wall clearance every 10 ms.
- **No tyre slip,** and a lateral grip of 3 m/s² is assumed in the profile, so this is planning evidence, not a driving result.

| Speed cap | MPC off | MPC apply (A2RL's latest presets) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0.3 m/s | flying lap 105.5 s, clearance ≥ 0.36 m | **103.4 s**, ≥ 0.31 m |
| 0.5 m/s | 63.4 s | **62.0 s** |
| 1.0 m/s | 31.8 s | **31.0 s** (also at 0.345 s pose age) |
| 1.5 m/s | yaw-error safe stop at s = 5.8 m | **20.7 s**, ≥ 0.31 m (also at 0.345 s) |
| 2.0 m/s | yaw-error safe stop at s = 3.4 m | **15.5 s**, ≥ 0.32 m (also at 0.345 s) |
| 3.0 m/s | contact | yaw-error safe stop at s = 10.8 m, no contact |

**Findings:**
- **The A2RL MPC is what makes speed possible here.** Its dead-time roll-forward compensates our ~0.3 s pose age; without it, B fails above 1 m/s.
- **In this model B laps up to 2 m/s.** For comparison, today's A lap takes 123 s at 0.3 m/s; B at the same cap takes 103 s, because it follows a smooth line instead of reacting to gaps.
- **Open for the speed stages (D33):**
  - real grip and slip in AutoDRIVE;
  - the stop distance at speed: braking from 2 m/s at 2 m/s² is 1 m plus 0.3–0.6 s of dead time;
  - the MPC's built-in 1 m/s model-speed floor (an A2RL full-size assumption, kept unchanged).
