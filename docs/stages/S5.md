## 11. D78 A fix: diagnosis, change and sim evidence; re-qualification run set (needs run approval)

**Correction to §5, §8 and §9.** I said H2 fails because the track narrows. **That was wrong.** H2's left gap is 1.06 m, and the pass needs the line moved only about 0.07 m. A per-replan trace of the lattice (`--trace`, added to the closed-loop sim) shows what actually happens. Evidence: `artifacts/s5/20261102T-d78-sim/diagnosis-*`.

**What actually happens in H2 (pre-fix planner):**
1. **The committed chain postpones the shift.** It stayed on the line to s = 6.0, then shifted 0.475 m over s = 6–8, starting less than 1 m before the box. AVLite's corridor check accepts this, because it models the car as centred on the path.
2. **The planner keeps it.** From 1.57 m out, the fresh lattice had clear, in-bounds, curvature-feasible left edges. AVLite's `should_switch_plan` still kept the committed chain, which looked clean and no shorter.
3. **The latch fires beside the box.** At s ≈ 6.2, every fresh level-0 edge starts at the car with zero lateral slope, so all of them clipped the box. The D58 latch counted that as "blocked" and stopped the car 0.22 m from it, although the plan being followed cleared the box.
4. **A second exposure, found while fixing.** While any agent is ahead, AVLite's overtake mode prefers lateral targets for every edge and relaxes the bounds. With a static box this pushed T2's tail edges to the far wall (body 0.151 m from the wall, against U3's 0.20 m) and back across the line (heading error 0.32 rad).

**Change** (`roboracer/planning/c0_lattice.py`, commit `7ffc5de`). It is a subclass of AVLite's planner; AVLite itself is unchanged.

| # | Change | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Early avoidance:** when the fresh line edge collides while the committed current edge still ends on the line, a clean lateral plan is taken at once | the shift starts about 1.6 m before the box, not < 1 m |
| 2 | **Gated D58 latch:** "no clear fresh edge" counts as blocked only while the followed plan runs into a confirmed obstacle within the horizon. Planner errors still count | no false latch beside a box being passed |
| 3 | **Stop-distance guard:** if the followed plan reaches an obstacle within the stop envelope, `blocked` latches at once. Envelope = D58's 0.30 m + 0.8 s × v + v²/(2 × 2.0), measured from the car's front | a late decision still ends ≥ 0.30 m short |
| 4 | **AVLite's overtake mode off** (`_agent_blocks_ahead` = False) | it is for moving cars; C0 obstacles are static |
| 5 | **Wall inset:** half the car width + 0.20 m (U3), not + 0.10 m. The racing line keeps ≥ 0.336 m nominal everywhere, so it stays in bounds | lateral edges keep U3 from the walls |

**Tried and not kept:**
- **Obstacle corridor margin 0.15–0.25 m:** at 0.15 m nothing changes; at 0.20–0.25 m the passes stop "blocked". The D57 margin of 0.10 m is kept.
- **`c28_preferred_extra_clearance` 0.15 m:** no change.
