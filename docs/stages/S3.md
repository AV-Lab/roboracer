## 2. The C0 plan

- **Reference:** the collider-derived centerline, 313 points at 0.1 m, lap 31.32 m; it starts at the spawn in the driving direction.
- **Line:** A2RL min-curvature line, 0.3 m margin from each edge, start pinned within ±0.05 m.
  - It moves at most **0.11 m** from the centerline, and the lap shortens to 31.25 m. On this narrow track (1.14–2.55 m wide) the centerline is already close to the min-curvature line.
  - Polyline clearance to the edge is 0.48 m.
  - An exactly converged solve (tighter solver tolerances, or the geometry scaled ×37 to A2RL's spacing) gives 0.113–0.114 m, so the default solver stopping slightly early does not matter.
- **Why the spacing must stay fine:** at 0.5 m spacing the line seemed to gain more (offset 0.49 m), but its segments cut the inner edges, to 0.26 m inside a 0.3 m margin, while every point met it. The plan builder now refuses such lines.
- **Truth isolation:** the plan is built from the S1-05 collider geometry, the same source as the scorer's. S3-01 still has to make the driving map a separate, validated asset (S3-04).
