## 1. Obstacle source — D54 A: virtual static obstacles (built; no motion)

**Decision:** "D54 A，用虚拟障碍物" (2026-10-29). The frozen player has no obstacle objects and no placement API (PRE-B09; I re-checked the source on 2026-10-28):
- the build contains only the `F1TENTH` scene (`level0`);
- the scene holds no obstacle objects;
- no script instantiates objects at runtime.

The simulator stays frozen (D18).

**Delivered:**
- **`roboracer/integration/virtual_obstacles.py`.** A fixture file holds world-frame polygons, with a provenance record (sha256); a tampered file is refused.
  - For each committed snapshot, the 1,081 LiDAR beams are ray-cast from the LiDAR origin at that snapshot's simulator pose. Each beam is cut to its first obstacle hit within the 10 m range.
  - No-return beams that hit an obstacle are filled in. Closer wall returns stay.
- **Bridge hook:** `AutoDriveBridge.obstacle_fixture`, applied inside `_offer` before anything reads the snapshot.
  - The raw-message recording stays unmodified. Replay through `_offer` applies the same fixture again.
  - `statistics()` reports the fixture name, its sha256 and the occluded-snapshot counts.
- **Scorer:** `roboracer_scoring.py score --obstacles <fixture>` reads the fixture independently, after the same sha256 check.
  - It reports `obstacle_contact_events` (swept-footprint overlap, labelled "not a physical collision") and `min_obstacle_clearance_m`, separately from wall contact and clearance.
  - Without `--obstacles`, every result is unchanged.

**Tests:** plugin 82/82 (6 new) and scripts 193/193 (2 new). They cover:
- the exact hit distance;
- pose rotation and translation;
- closer returns kept, and no-return beams filled;
- fixture integrity and validation;
- the bridge path with Devkit-shaped messages, with the raw recording unmodified;
- no driving module references the fixture;
- scorer obstacle contact and clearance, with wall results unchanged, and its tamper refusal.

**Open for S4-04:**
- **Truth isolation with a fixture.** The fixture must use the simulator pose, as any simulated sensor does. The proof for S4 will perturb the stack-visible truth after the fixture is applied, and show B's outputs unchanged, once obstacle detection is in the stack.
- **Scan/pose skew.** Up to 0.06 s (measured about 0.04 s). This is about 0.02 m of obstacle displacement at 0.5 m/s.

## 3. Lattice at C0 scale and the blocked stop (built); closed-loop kinematic check passed (no motion)

**`C0LatticePlanner`** (`roboracer/planning/c0_lattice.py`) is AVLite's `GreedyLatticePlanner`, unchanged, with two additions:
- **C0 settings.** Every changed AVLite default is listed with its reason in `C0_PLANNING` and recorded in `settings_applied`. AVLite's values are sized for a full-size car:
  - **maneuvers:** 2 m edges instead of 30 m;
  - **clearances:** corridor and boundary clearance of half the car width + 0.10 m (D57);
  - **minimum curvature speed:** 0.3 m/s instead of 3 m/s;
  - **sampling:** even and deterministic instead of random;
  - **ego box:** 0.526 × 0.288 m instead of AVLite's 4.5 × 2.0 m, which the collision corridor would otherwise use.
- **D58 latched stop.** No collision-free, in-bounds level-0 edge, or a replan that raises, counts as a blocked replan.
  - Two consecutive blocked replans latch `blocked`. The local plan it then hands out has zero velocity: a new plan on the same path, so the committed or global trajectory is never modified.
  - Only `reset()` (rearm) clears the latch.

**Closed-loop check** (`scripts/roboracer_s4_closedloop_sim.py`; evidence `artifacts/s4/20261029T060139Z-closedloop-sim/`):
- **Stack:** the whole S4 stack, assembled through AVLite's `executor_factory` as the runner would:
  - `PerceptionPipeline` → `ScanObstacleDetection`;
  - `MapIcpLocalization`, with the simulator pose filtered out and truth withheld;
  - `RaceLineGlobalPlanner` → `C0LatticePlanner`;
  - `RacingLineController` in `selected` mode;
  - all ticked by `SyncExecuter.step` every 0.1 s.
- **World:** a subclass of the plugin's own `AutoDriveBridge`. Scans are ray-cast against the C0 walls with 5 mm range noise (fixed seed), and the fixture is applied through the bridge hook.
- **Timing:** a new snapshot every 0.18 s, 0.3 s old.
- **Plant:** S3's kinematic bicycle.
- **Scoring:** independently, from the plant state every 10 ms.

| Case | Outcome | Lap | Min wall clearance | Obstacle clearance | ICP |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| no obstacle | lap | 63.8 s | 0.291 m | — | valid 638/638 |
| **T1** | **passed left**, lap | 64.6 s | 0.291 m | **0.512 m** | valid 646/646 |
| **T2** | **passed right**, lap | 64.4 s | 0.281 m | **0.557 m** | valid 644/644 |
| **T3** | **blocked latched** at 8.2 s; at rest **1.416 m** before the barrier | — | 0.366 m | 1.416 m | valid 110/110 |

No contact and no off-track in any case. The T3 stop is outside the 0.93 m where ICP would fail (§2).

**Tick budget.** The first settings (3 levels × 7 samples, 105 edges per replan) took p50 69–82 ms and max **101 ms** per tick, against the 100 ms tick. A profile shows 94 ms in AVLite's lattice construction: quintic edges 46 ms, boundary checks 26 ms, collision checks 20 ms. ICP takes 1.7 ms, detection 0.6 ms, the controller 1.9 ms. With 2 levels × 5 samples (30 edges, 4 m look-ahead), the tick is **p50 18–23 ms, max 31 ms**. T2's wall clearance rose from 0.18 to 0.28 m with the coarser lattice. Both runs are kept in the evidence.

**This is planning evidence, not a driving result:** kinematic model, no tyre slip, synthesized scans.

**Tests:** plugin 87/87 (4 new lattice tests: C0 settings and ego box, open track never blocked, latch after two replans and cleared only by reset, shared trajectory untouched, planner error counts as blocked) and scripts 193/193.

**Finding (D61): an obstacle near the walls biases ICP along-track, undetected.**
- **What happens.** Obstacle returns within ICP's 0.3 m correspondence range of the wall map get matched to the walls.

- **Where it does not happen.** At the real T1/T2 geometry (box 0.31 m from the wall), ICP error is the same with and without the fixture: max 0.015 m both ways.
- **Why it matters.** In T3 the car is stopped, so the error does not move it. But after rearm it would start 5–10 cm wrong, and the S3 validity checks do not see it.

**Finding: T1/T2 do not force a manoeuvre.** At 0.45 m off the line, the box's near face is 0.325 m from the line, outside the 0.244 m corridor. The racing line itself already clears the box. **Stage `s4`:**
- **Family:** a B/ICP stage with the **V1 envelope (D59)** in all three tables (runner, supervisor, plan stage): 0.5 m/s, 0.35 rad, `u_max` 0.12, overspeed abort 0.7 m/s. - **Fixture:** `--obstacle-fixture` is required for `s4` and refused for every other stage. The run's status records the fixture's path and sha256 and the lattice settings.
- **D56 freshness:** the run aborts if the detector produces no new obstacle result for **0.5 s** while driving.
  - The age is measured from when the result was produced, the same basis as the observation-gap rule.

- **D58 blocked outcome:** once the lattice latches "blocked", B's target is 0. After **2 s** at rest the episode ends with `finish_reason = "blocked"`, recording the reason, time, distance and speed when latched.
- **Unchanged monitors:** workspace precheck, forward and side clearance, containment and overspeed. They read the bridge's scan, so they see the virtual obstacles too.
- **Scoring:** `roboracer_episode.py` reads the fixture from the run's own record and scores obstacle contact and clearance (hash-checked). `contact_free_and_on_track` now also requires no obstacle contact.
- **Interlock:** the runner and the supervisor refuse `s4` until the approval record lists `S4`. It does not yet.
