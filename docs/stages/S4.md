## 1. Obstacle source — D54 A: virtual static obstacles (built; no motion)

**Decision:** "D54 A，用虚拟障碍物" (2026-10-29). The frozen player has no obstacle objects and no placement API (PRE-B09; I re-checked the source on 2026-10-28):
- the build contains only the `F1TENTH` scene (`level0`);
- the scene holds no obstacle objects;
- no script instantiates objects at runtime.

The simulator stays frozen (D18).

**Delivered:**
- **`roboracer/integration/virtual_obstacles.py`.** A fixture file holds world-frame polygons, with a provenance record (sha256); a tampered file is refused.
  - For each committed snapshot, the 1,081 LiDAR beams are ray-cast from the LiDAR origin at that snapshot's simulator pose. Each beam is cut to its first obstacle hit within the 10 m range.
  - No-return beams that hit an obstacle are filled in. Closer wall returns stay.
- **Bridge hook:** `AutoDriveBridge.obstacle_fixture`, applied inside `_offer` before anything reads the snapshot.
  - The raw-message recording stays unmodified. Replay through `_offer` applies the same fixture again.
  - `statistics()` reports the fixture name, its sha256 and the occluded-snapshot counts.
- **Scorer:** `roboracer_scoring.py score --obstacles <fixture>` reads the fixture independently, after the same sha256 check.
  - It reports `obstacle_contact_events` (swept-footprint overlap, labelled "not a physical collision") and `min_obstacle_clearance_m`, separately from wall contact and clearance.
  - Without `--obstacles`, every result is unchanged.

**Tests:** plugin 82/82 (6 new) and scripts 193/193 (2 new). They cover:
- the exact hit distance;
- pose rotation and translation;
- closer returns kept, and no-return beams filled;
- fixture integrity and validation;
- the bridge path with Devkit-shaped messages, with the raw recording unmodified;
- no driving module references the fixture;
- scorer obstacle contact and clearance, with wall results unchanged, and its tamper refusal.

**Open for S4-04:**
- **Truth isolation with a fixture.** The fixture must use the simulator pose, as any simulated sensor does. The proof for S4 will perturb the stack-visible truth after the fixture is applied, and show B's outputs unchanged, once obstacle detection is in the stack.
- **Scan/pose skew.** Up to 0.06 s (measured about 0.04 s). This is about 0.02 m of obstacle displacement at 0.5 m/s.

## 2. D55–D60 confirmed; fixtures, the selected-path port and obstacle detection (built; offline, no motion)

**Decisions:** D55–D60 confirmed ("确认", 2026-10-29).

**Fixtures (D55)** (`scripts/roboracer_s4_fixtures.py` → `scripts/s4-fixtures/`):
- Built on the committed V1 racing line, each with a provenance record (sha256, inputs, builder hash).
- Each is checked against the scorer's drivable region. The car's footprint is 0.288 m wide, so a passable gap needs 0.488 m (footprint + 2 × 0.10 m):

| Fixture | Obstacle | Gap left / right | Passable |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `t1-left-pass` | 0.25 m box at s = 7.5 m, 0.45 m right of the line | 1.22 / 0.31 m | left only |
| `t2-right-pass` | the mirror | 0.32 / 1.21 m | right only |
| `t3-blocked` | 0.20 m deep barrier at s = 5.0 m, reaching 0.2 m into both walls | 0 / 0 m | none |

**Selected-path tracking (D57)** (`2f0363a`):
- A2RL `trajectory_reference_mode` is ported into `RacingLineController`: "selected" tracks the local path, "global" its parent (the S3 behaviour, still the default).
- The equivalence test against A2RL shows identical steering in both modes, and that the modes differ.
- The six A2RL files ported in S3 are byte-identical between `0b5cfa8` and `fe2fc01`.

**Obstacle detection (D56)** (`roboracer/perception/scan_obstacles.py`, `ScanObstacleDetection`, a subclass of AVLite `FastBEVLidarDetection`):
- **Inputs:** the stack's ICP pose and the scan; nothing else.
- **Wall subtraction:** points within 0.05 m of the S3 wall map are removed.
- **Segmentation at C0 scale:** 0.10 m gap; cluster extent 0.05–3.0 m.
- **Quality:** ≥ 4 points per cluster, confirmed on 2 consecutive scans.
- **Freshness:** each result is stamped with its scan time.
- **Correction to the proposal's table:** obstacles go into `agent_vehicles` as stationary agents, the list AVLite's lattice collision check reads. It does not read `static_obstacles`.
- **Deviation from AVLite:** an edge-on cluster keeps its observed face length, and only the unseen depth (0.25 m) is defaulted. AVLite substitutes a 4.5 × 2.0 m car box, which would shrink the T3 barrier and could open a false gap.

**Offline check** (`scripts/roboracer_s4_offline.py`; evidence `artifacts/s4/20261029T054851Z-offline-detection/`; open-loop replay of the S2 laps used for S3-04, through the plugin's bridge with truth withheld, ICP and the detector):

| Case | Result |
| --- | --- |
| No obstacles, 2 laps (1,662 scans) | **0 phantom detections**; ICP valid on every scan |
| T1, 2 laps | first confirmed at **7.4 m**; confirmed on 132/133 and 137/138 scans before the car reaches it; centre error p50 5 mm, max 0.13 m; no false positives; ICP valid |
| T2, 1 lap | first confirmed at 7.4 m; no false positives; ICP valid all lap. The recorded path never nears this box, so the "before reaching" window is the whole lap and its scores include scans with the box behind. |
| T3, 2 laps | first confirmed at **4.9 m** (the whole straight); ICP **invalid from 0.93 m, lost from 0.81 m** |

**Finding: a full blockage costs ICP inliers.**
- The barrier's returns are outliers to the wall map. The inlier fraction falls from 0.87 at 2.3 m to 0.71 at 1.0 m, and the frozen S3 validity limit trips at 0.93 m.
- The one T3 "false positive" is the barrier itself, placed 0.38 m off after the loss.
- **Consequence for D58:** B must decide "blocked" and have stopped well outside 1 m from a full blockage. Stopped at about 1.5 m, the inlier fraction stays near 0.8. The S3 ICP limits are not changed.
- In replay, ICP is also lost after the car reaches T1/T2. There the recorded car drives *through* the virtual box (LiDAR inside the polygon), a state that cannot occur physically.

**Tests:** plugin 83/83 (adds the selected/global equivalence test; the S1-01 export list now includes `ScanObstacleDetection`) and scripts 193/193.
